MacSync

How to install the same apps on all your Macs

Install MacSync on each Mac. It lists the apps your other Macs have and this one doesn't, and installs them: Homebrew apps in one click, App Store apps from their page, and others from their developer.

Download MacSync Free, macOS 13 or later.

Step by step

  1. Install MacSync on each Mac, with iCloud Drive on.
  2. Open the sync window and look at Apps on your other Macs.
  3. Press Get on one app, or Get All.
  4. Switch off any app a Mac doesn't need; MacSync remembers.

Where the apps come from

Never from your other Mac. Homebrew installs its own, the App Store its own, and the rest are downloaded from where their developer publishes them and checked against their checksum or signature.

Keeping them up to date

MacSync updates Homebrew apps and tools, npm and uv packages, and apps that update themselves. An app you have open is updated after you quit it.

Removing an app everywhere

Remove from All Macs uninstalls it here, and each other Mac asks before removing its own copy.
