How to sync the Dock between Macs

macOS has no built-in Dock sync. Install MacSync, a free menu bar app, on each Mac with iCloud Drive on and the same Apple Account. A Dock change on one Mac is saved within seconds, and the others take the same apps in the same order when you open the lid or press Apply.

Download MacSync Free, macOS 13 or later.

Step by step

  1. Turn on iCloud Drive on both Macs, signed in to the same Apple Account.
  2. Install MacSync on both and let it open at login.
  3. Arrange the Dock on one Mac.
  4. On the other, open the lid, or click MacSync in the menu bar, Review Changes and Apply.

What comes across

The apps and their order, icon size, the Dock's position, and Safari web apps. An app a Mac doesn't have is skipped there and offered to install, so you never get a question mark in the Dock.

Keeping one Mac different

Switch the Dock section off on that Mac. You can also choose whether the last change wins or one Mac always does.

Undoing it

A restore point is saved before every Apply. Undo goes back one; History goes back to any of the last 20.
