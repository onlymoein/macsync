MacSync

How to sync Mac settings between two computers

MacSync copies the macOS settings you choose (trackpad, keyboard, Finder, Dock behaviour, hot corners, screenshots and more) between your Macs through your own iCloud Drive. Each change shows the old value next to the new one, and nothing changes until you press Apply.

Download MacSync Free, macOS 13 or later.

Step by step

  1. Install MacSync on both Macs.
  2. Change a setting on one Mac as usual, in System Settings.
  3. On the other, open Settings in the sync window and compare old and new.
  4. Switch off anything that should stay different, and press Apply.

What never syncs

Passwords, tokens, API keys, license keys, SSH and GPG keys, shell history and .env files. A config file with a key in it stays on its Mac.

Good to know

Some trackpad, keyboard and appearance settings need you to log out once before they take full effect.
